Up Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Up is an Olympic weightlifting journal for logging training sessions, attempts, personal records, bodyweight, competition attempts, and related progress analytics.

Information You Enter

Training entries, lifts, attempts, competition records, bodyweight entries, notes, and personal records are stored by the app for app functionality. Do not enter information you do not want stored in your training journal.

Purchases

Subscription purchases are handled through Apple and RevenueCat. Up may receive purchase history and entitlement status so the app can unlock Pro features, restore purchases, and maintain access.

App Interaction And Diagnostics

Up may process product interaction and diagnostic information for app functionality, troubleshooting, and reliability. These data types are not used to identify you personally by Up.

AI Coach

If you use AI Coach, the message you submit is sent to the app's AI service provider so a response can be generated. Do not include sensitive personal, medical, or confidential information in AI Coach prompts.

Data Sharing

Up does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers that support subscriptions, diagnostics, AI responses, hosting, or Apple platform services.

Children

Up is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact support so it can be reviewed.
