Privacy Policy

Galley · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Galley does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server that stores your business information. Your events, clients, venues, menus, recipes, costs, quotes, prep plans, dietary lists, notes and profile are never sent to us. The only thing that ever leaves your device for our server is a question you ask the AI Catering Guide, and only after you allow it (see below).

AI Catering Guide

Galley Pro includes the AI Catering Guide, which answers catering questions. It is off until you allow it: before your first question, Galley shows what is sent and asks you to choose Allow or Not now. Nothing is sent before you tap Allow, and after that only when you tap Send or a suggested question.

What is sent, each time you ask: the question exactly as you typed it; Galley's fixed instructions (answer as general catering advice, keep food-safety answers general and point you to your local rules, never call a dish safe for an allergy); and Galley's built-in catering reference data (portion guides, food cost benchmarks, prep timelines, dietary and food-safety tables), which is the same for every user. Your earlier questions are not sent again. Please leave client names, addresses and anything else private out of your questions.

Never sent: your events, clients, venues, menus, recipes, costs, quotes, prep plans, dietary lists and notes, and your name, Apple Account or email address.

Where it goes: the question travels over an encrypted connection to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI, the company behind Grok, and returns the answer to you. Our server keeps no copy. xAI handles the request under its own privacy policy. Answers are general catering information and can be wrong; for food safety and allergies, follow your local rules and check every label. Your questions and the answers stay in the chat in Galley (and in your iCloud, see below) until you clear them.

You can turn the AI Catering Guide off at any time in Profile > AI Catering Guide; while it is off, nothing is sent.

Data Storage

Your events, recipes, profile and AI chat are stored on your device. When your device is signed in to iCloud, Galley also keeps them in your private iCloud database so they stay in sync between your devices; that database belongs to your Apple Account and we cannot read it. To keep your data on one device only, turn off iCloud for Galley in your device's iCloud settings. Event reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Event sheets and P&L reports are PDF files made on your device and go only where you choose to share them.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in with Apple is optional and only adds your name to your Galley profile. Galley keeps the identifier Apple provides on your device; it does not use or keep your email address. Your events and recipes sync through iCloud whether or not you sign in.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

xAI (Grok) — answers the AI Catering Guide, only after you allow it, from the text described above. Policy

Apple — payments, iCloud sync of your data, and Sign in with Apple if you use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Galley are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Galley, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Galley. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Galley is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all your data in Profile > Delete All Data: it removes your events (with their menus, prep plans and dietary lists), recipes, profile and AI chat from your device and from your iCloud, removes every scheduled reminder, and resets your AI choice. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account does the same and clears the stored Apple identifier; to stop Galley using your Apple Account, remove it in Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple. Deleting the app alone leaves the iCloud copy in place. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
