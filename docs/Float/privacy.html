Privacy Policy

Float · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Float is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Float, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Float is stored on your device.

AI Features

Some features in Float use artificial intelligence. When you use one, your request - the text you type and the Float information needed to answer it, such as your entries and statistics - is sent to xAI (Grok) to generate the answer. This happens only when you use an AI feature.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content. We don't add your Apple ID or email address to these requests, and we don't keep copies of them. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). Please don't enter information you wouldn't want to share with the AI provider.

We process this to provide the feature you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email address. They stay on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on); we don't run an account server.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Float are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Float, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Float. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Float contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Float is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete the data on your device by deleting the app. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Float sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
