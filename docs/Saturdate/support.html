Getting started

How do we pair our phones?

On one phone, open the Us tab and send the invite. Your partner taps Join on Saturdate's welcome screen (or, if they already use Saturdate, Us → Have a code?) and types the code. From then on you see the same plans, map and memories, and anything they had before comes along.

Where do our Saturdays start?

From the town you set when you started. To change it, tap Us → Saturdays start in.

Saturdate Plus

Does my partner need to subscribe too?

No. One subscription covers both of you while you are paired, even if it was bought on the other phone.

How do I restore a purchase?

Tap Restore Purchases on the Saturdate Plus screen, or in the Us tab.

How do I cancel?

In iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Saturdate. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

On the day

Can I see the day on my lock screen?

Yes. When it is the day, Saturdate shows a Live Activity with the time to the next stop. If it doesn't appear, check iOS Settings → Saturdate → Live Activities.

Does it work without signal?

Your planned day is saved on your phone, so the timeline and stop details work without signal. Directions open in Apple Maps.

Memories

Why can't I read my partner's note?

Notes are revealed once you have both written yours about the day.

Can I add a Saturday we went on before?

Yes: Our Map → the + button → Add a past Saturdate.

How do I share a day?

Open its postcard and tap Share recap. You get slides ready for TikTok's photo mode or your Story.

How do we stop sharing a map?

Us → Leave the pair. The shared map stays with your partner and you start again on your own.

Your data

The Us tab lets you delete all of your data, or your whole account. The Privacy Policy explains what each one removes.
