Privacy Policy

Saturdate · Last updated October 19, 2026

Saturdate plans days out for two people and keeps the ones you have been on as a shared map. It is made by Karol Jaworski ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions about this policy or your data: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

  • We store what the two of you put into Saturdate so it is on both your phones.
  • Your plans are written with the help of AI from details about the day and the places, never from your names, your home or your photos.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your data or your whole account from inside the app, at any time.

What we store, and why

Your account

When you first open Saturdate it creates an account with a random identifier, so your Saturdays survive a new phone or a reinstall. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an identifier for you and, only if you choose to share it, your first name. We do not ask Apple for your email address.

The two of you

The first names you enter, your home area (saved rounded to about 1 km, used as the starting point for drive times), your answers to the This-or-That questions, how often you would like a Saturdate, and your Someday list of places.

Your Saturdays

The days dealt to you, which one each of you picked, and each day's timeline.

Your memories

The photos you choose to add to a memory, with the time and place they were taken so they land in the right spot on your map; your "best part" notes; and past Saturdates you add by hand. Photos are re-saved by the app before upload, without the original file's other metadata.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your phone's push token, its language and which kinds of notifications you want, so we can tell you when your partner picks a day, adds photos or writes their note.

Saturdate Plus

When you subscribe, Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app sends Apple's signed record of the purchase to our server, which checks it with Apple's certificates and gives Plus to both of you.

Technical logs

Our hosting providers keep short-lived request logs, including IP addresses, to keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We process this data to provide Saturdate to you (the contract between us), and, for logs and security, in our legitimate interest in running a safe service. Notifications are sent only with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.

What stays on your phone

  • Location. If you choose "Use my location" when setting your home, Saturdate asks for your location once to find your town. Only the rounded home area is saved. On the day of a Saturdate, if you turn on "Follow the day on the map", Saturdate uses your location while the app is open to show you on the day's map and tick off each stop as you reach it; that location is never sent to us or anyone else. Saturdate never uses your location in the background.
  • Your photo library. To suggest the photos you took on the day, Saturdate looks at your library on your phone. Only the photos you add are uploaded. Saving recap slides needs permission to add photos, nothing more.
  • Drive times, maps and weather are requested from Apple directly by your phone (see below).
  • Widgets and the Live Activity read a small copy of your next Saturdate and a few memory photos kept on your phone.

Your partner

Pairing creates a shared space. Your partner sees your names, your plans and picks, the memories, the photos either of you adds, and your "best part" note once they have written theirs. Database rules keep a couple's data visible only to its two members.

Who else receives data

We use these providers to run Saturdate. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Supabase (database, photo storage and sign-in), with servers in Ireland (EU). Everything listed above is stored here.
  • xAI (Grok) writes the text of your plans and short place descriptions. For a plan it receives the date and season, your mood, whether a dog is coming, the destination and nearby places, and the drive time. It never receives your names, your home, your photos or your account.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, Apple Maps (maps and drive times between your home area and the places), WeatherKit (forecasts for the places you may visit) and App Store purchases. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat receives your subscription status and purchase events under an anonymous identifier, so we can understand how Saturdate Plus is used. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Google Cloud runs the tool that builds our catalogue of places for a region. It receives only a map area, never who asked for it.
  • Google Maps Platform (Places) shows the coffee and meal spots on your plan. When a plan has a coffee stop or a meal, your phone asks Google for well-rated places near that stop and shows Google's own place card. Google receives the search and the area around the stop, and its SDK collects usage and device information as described in Google's privacy policy. We store the chosen spot's Google place ID with your plan, and its map position for 30 days, after which it is deleted.
  • Open data: place facts and photos come from Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and OpenStreetMap-based datasets. Nothing about you is sent to them.

Some of these providers (xAI, RevenueCat, Apple) may process data in the United States; where that happens, data is protected by the safeguards in their data processing terms, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell or rent personal data, show ads, or share data for advertising, and Saturdate does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists, so your map is there when you come back to it. Push tokens are removed as soon as Apple reports them as no longer valid. Hosting logs are kept for a short period and then deleted.

Deleting your data

  • Us → Delete all our data deletes your memories, photos, notes, plans and Someday list, for both of you. Your accounts and your pairing stay.
  • Us → Delete account deletes your account and every photo and note you added, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. If you were not paired, everything goes. If you were paired, the plans and memories you shared stay with your partner, without your photos and notes.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete what is stored on our servers; use the options above, or email us.

If you used Route

Saturdate replaces an earlier app called Route. Route kept your trips on your phone and, if you used iCloud, in your private iCloud, which only you control (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud). When you first open Saturdate, it offers to add your past trips to your map. If you agree, each trip's name, date and destination, and the first highlight from its journal, become a memory as described above, with a satellite image of the place from Apple Maps as its stamp. Route's copy on your phone is deleted once your trips are on the map, or when you choose to leave them out (if a trip can't be added, for example without a connection, Saturdate asks again next time). Nothing from Route is uploaded unless you agree.

Children

Saturdate is made for adults planning days out together. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU, the UK and California), you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy of it, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Photos are stored privately and are reachable only through short-lived links issued to members of your couple.

Changes

When what Saturdate collects or shares changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
