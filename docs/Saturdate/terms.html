What Saturdate does

Saturdate suggests day trips for two, written with the help of AI from open data about places, travel times from Apple Maps and forecasts from Apple Weather. You can pick a day together, follow it on the day, and keep the days you went on as a shared map of memories.

Plans are suggestions

Places change. Opening hours, access, prices, trail conditions, water quality and weather can differ from what the app shows, and AI-written text can be wrong. Before you go and while you are out:

  • check that places are open and accessible, and follow local rules and signs;
  • judge for yourselves whether an activity, a swim, a hike or the weather is safe for you;
  • never use the app while driving, and follow traffic laws.

You are responsible for your decisions and your safety on the day.
