Privacy Policy

Curio · Last updated: August 13, 2026

Data We Collect

Curio collects minimal anonymous data to operate the app and process subscriptions. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifying information unless you choose to Sign in with Apple.

Data Storage

Your collection data (objects, photos, provenance records) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you enable iCloud sync, data is also stored in your private iCloud account — accessible only to you.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose to Sign in with Apple, we store only an anonymous user identifier provided by Apple. Your name and email are never required and are not stored on our servers.

Subscriptions

Curio Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. Purchase history is managed by Apple's StoreKit framework and is not stored on our servers. We use RevenueCat in observer mode solely to track subscription status for app functionality — no personal data is shared.

AI Features

If you use the AI Collection Guide (Curio Pro), your questions are sent to our Cloudflare Worker proxy, which forwards them to xAI Grok. Questions are not linked to your identity and are not stored after processing.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — Subscription analytics (observer mode only). Policy

Apple iCloud / CloudKit — Private data sync. Policy

xAI Grok (AI Guide only) — Question processing via our proxy. Policy

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data. Curio is an offline-first tool — all core features work without an internet connection.

Children

Curio is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all your collection data within the app via Profile → Delete All Data. Delete your account via Profile → Delete Account. You may also delete the app to remove all local data. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
