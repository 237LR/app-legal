Privacy Policy

Grove · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Grove does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own for your records. Your trees, their pruning, pest and disease, and harvest logs, tree photos, journal notes, rainfall, tasks and orchard profile stay on your device.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Grove does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. Tree photos you add are stored on the device with the tree. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in the app. If you tap "Detect my zone", Grove asks for your location once and looks up your USDA hardiness zone on the device; the location is not stored or sent anywhere, only the zone you keep. Reminders (monthly tasks, seasonal alerts and harvest reminders) are local notifications scheduled on your device.

AI Orchard Guide (optional, Pro)

The AI Orchard Guide answers questions about fruit trees using xAI's Grok. Grove asks for your permission before the first question and sends nothing until you allow it. You can turn it off at any time in Profile > AI Guide; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent: the question you type (or the suggested question you tap), only when you send it; a short summary of your orchard: your hardiness zone, your hemisphere, how many trees you track and how many years you have been growing; the variety and species of up to 8 of your trees (for example "Honeycrisp Apple"); and Grove's standard instructions for the answer (general information only, never pesticide or fertilizer rates, point to the product label and the local extension office). Your earlier questions in the chat are not sent again. Your conversations are saved on your device so you can return to them.

What is never sent: your notes and journal, tree photos, your pruning, pest and disease, and harvest records, rainfall, where your trees stand, rootstocks, your orchard's name, your location, and your name, Apple ID or email address.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the answer; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the answer under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. Answers are general information, not professional advice.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — writes the AI Orchard Guide's answers from your question and orchard summary, only after you allow it. Policy

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Grove are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Grove, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Grove. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Grove is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (it removes every tree, log, photo, note, rainfall reading, task, AI Guide conversation and your orchard profile, and cancels pending reminders), or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes your Apple ID details from the app; to stop using Sign in with Apple for Grove, go to iOS Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple.
