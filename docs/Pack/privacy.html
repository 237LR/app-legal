Privacy Policy

Pack · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Pack does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own for your records. The children you add (name, age, grade, school, colour, allergies and restrictions, loves and dislikes, notes), your lunch plans, the eat results you log and your settings stay on your device.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Pack does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in the app.

AI Lunch Ideas (optional, Pro)

AI Lunch Ideas suggests five lunches for one child using xAI's Grok. Pack is used by parents, and it asks the parent for permission before the first request; nothing is sent until you tap Allow. You can turn the feature off at any time in Profile > AI Lunch Ideas; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent, each time you tap Generate Ideas: the child's age (if you set one); the allergies and restrictions you recorded for that child, so the ideas avoid them; the foods that child usually eats (eaten 70% of the time or more) and usually leaves (under 30%), from the results you logged; the foods you typed under Loves and Dislikes for that child; the foods logged in the last 7 days; and Pack's standard instructions (five complete lunches, avoid every recorded allergy, keep it practical).

What is never sent: your child's name (the request says "your child", and Pack removes the name from anything you typed before sending), grade, school, notes for the packer and sandwich cut; your other children's details; the dates of your lunch plans; and your own name, Apple ID or email address.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the ideas; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the ideas under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. AI ideas are suggestions, not advice: please check every one against your child's allergies before you pack it.

Reminders

If you turn on Reminders in Profile, Pack schedules them as local notifications on your device (a weekly prep reminder, and a midweek nudge when the last school days are not planned yet). They are created on the device from your own plans; nothing is sent to a server.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — writes AI Lunch Ideas from what is listed above, only after you allow it. Policy

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, notifications, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Pack are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Pack, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Pack. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Pack is used by parents to plan their children's lunches; it is not directed at children under 13. The details a parent enters about a child stay on the device, except the items listed under AI Lunch Ideas, which are sent only with the parent's permission and never include the child's name.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes the stored link to your Apple ID and resets your AI Lunch Ideas and Reminders choices. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
