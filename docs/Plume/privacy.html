Privacy Policy

Plume · Last updated: July 17, 2026

Plume is a study app that turns your notes into study material. This policy explains what we collect, how it is stored, and the third parties that process it.

What We Collect

The content you create or import — your notes, the text you capture or paste, study answers, exams, and your study activity (scores, streaks, progress). If you create an account, we also process your email address for sign-in.

How It's Stored

Your content is stored locally on your device. If you sign in, it is also synced to our secure backend (Supabase) so your study material is available across your devices. We do not store your content for any purpose other than running the app for you.

AI Processing

Features such as note generation, answer grading, the tutor, practice questions, mock exams, and homework help work by sending the text you provide (your notes, questions, problems, or captured/imported text) to our AI provider, xAI (Grok), through our backend, which returns the generated result. Only the text needed to produce the result is sent. See xAI's Privacy Policy for how they handle data they process.

So these features work — and so free usage can be limited fairly — the app creates an anonymous, device-linked account with our backend the first time you use an AI feature, even before you sign in. It holds a random identifier and your AI-usage counts; it is not your email or name and is not linked to your identity unless you choose to sign in. It is used only to run the service and apply usage limits, never for advertising or tracking.

On-Device Processing

Lecture and voice transcription (Speech Recognition) and reading text from photos (Vision) are performed on your device. Your audio recordings and photos are not uploaded for these features — only the resulting text becomes part of your note, which is then handled as described above.

Third-Party Services

Supabase — account, secure storage, and sync. Policy
xAI (Grok) — AI processing of the text you submit. Policy
Apple — subscriptions and in-app purchases. Policy

No Tracking, No Ads, No Sale

We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

Children

Plume is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete your account and its synced data from inside the app (Profile → Delete account). Deleting the app removes the data stored locally on your device. For any request, contact karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
