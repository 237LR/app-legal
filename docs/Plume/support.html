FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?Open Plume → Profile → Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel?iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Plume → Cancel.
Where is my data stored?On your device, and — if you sign in — synced to your secure Plume account so it's available across devices.
How do I delete my data?In the app, go to Profile → Delete account to remove your synced data. Deleting the app removes the data stored locally on your device.
Is the AI always correct?No — AI-generated notes, answers, and worked solutions are study aids and can be wrong. Always double-check important material against your own sources.
