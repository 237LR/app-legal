Privacy Policy

Beta · Last updated: October 10, 2026

Beta is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Beta, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Beta is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, it is also synced to your private iCloud account, which Apple operates and we cannot access.

AI Features

The AI Climbing Coach (part of Beta Pro) sends, with each question: the question you type, the last 20 messages of the chat (your questions and the coach's answers), a short summary of your 5 latest sessions (gym or outdoor, the date, how many problems you sent out of how many you logged, and the hardest grade you sent) and up to 3 active projects (the project's name and target grade). Your injury log and training log, the names of your gyms and crags, session, route and attempt notes, wall angles, hold types, ratings and durations, and your name, email and Apple Account are never sent. Beta asks you before the first question and sends nothing until you tap Allow; you can switch the AI Climbing Coach off at any time in Profile > Settings > AI Climbing Coach. Answers are general information, not medical advice. It goes to xAI (Grok), which writes the answer.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content. We don't add your Apple ID or email address to these requests, and we don't keep copies of them. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). Please don't enter information you wouldn't want to share with the AI provider.

We process this because you asked for it and allowed it (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and (b)); you can withdraw your permission at any time in Profile > Settings > AI Climbing Coach.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email address. They stay on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on); we don't run an account server.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Beta are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Beta, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Beta. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Beta contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Beta is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete the data on your device by deleting the app and data synced to iCloud from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Beta sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
