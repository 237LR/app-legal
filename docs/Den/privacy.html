Privacy Policy

Den · Last updated: April 25, 2026

Data You Enter

Den stores the information you choose to add to the app, including your dog's profile, potty logs, crate sessions, naps, walks, barking notes, medication notes, and routine settings.

Data Storage

Your Den data is stored on your device using Apple's local storage frameworks. If iCloud sync is enabled on your device, your data may also sync to your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit service.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions and purchase status are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Den also uses RevenueCat in observer mode to help confirm subscription status and purchase events. Den does not use third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

No Tracking or Sale of Data

Den does not track you across apps or websites, does not sell your personal data, and does not use advertising identifiers for profiling.

Data Deletion

You can delete your in-app data from Den's settings. Removing the app also removes local data from the device. If iCloud sync is enabled, related records may remain in your private iCloud account until deleted there.
