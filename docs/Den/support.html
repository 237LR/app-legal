FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?Open Den, go to Settings, then tap Restore Purchases.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Den.
Is my data backed up?Den stores data on-device and can sync through your private iCloud account when iCloud is enabled.
How do I delete my data?Use Delete All Data inside Den's settings. Removing the app also clears local on-device data.
