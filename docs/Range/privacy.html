Privacy Policy

Range · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Range does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own. Your practice sessions, range tests, songs and daily voice check-ins are never sent to us.

Microphone

Range uses the microphone only while a practice session or a range test is open, to work out which note you are singing. The sound is analysed on your device as it comes in and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. If you turn microphone access off, the sessions and the range test still open, without live pitch.

Data Storage

Your practice history, range tests, songs and check-ins are stored on your device. When your device is signed in to iCloud, Range also keeps them in your private iCloud database so they stay in sync between your devices; that database belongs to your Apple Account and we cannot read it. To keep your data on one device only, turn off iCloud for Range in your device's iCloud settings. The voice type you pick during setup and your reminder settings stay on your device. If you use Sign in with Apple, the first name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are stored on your device, only to show your name in the app.

Notifications

The daily warmup reminder, if you turn it on, is scheduled on your device as a local notification. Nothing is sent to us to deliver it.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, iCloud sync of your data, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Range are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Range, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Range. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Range is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data: it removes your practice history, range tests, songs and check-ins from your devices and from your iCloud. Deleting the app alone leaves the iCloud copy in place. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also deletes your data and removes your Apple ID details from the app. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
