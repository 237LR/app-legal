FAQ

How do I back up my book, or move it to my iPad or a new iPhone?Settings (the gear on Today) › Back Up or Restore › Back Up the Book makes one .polishbook file with every client, visit, appointment, photo, amount and supply. Save it to Files or AirDrop it to your iPad or new iPhone. The file holds your clients' allergies and medical notes, so keep it somewhere only you can open. Polish doesn't sync through iCloud, so a backup is how your book moves.
How do I restore a backup?Tap the .polishbook file in Files, AirDrop or Mail and Polish opens its restore sheet, or use Settings › Back Up or Restore › Restore. Choose Add What's Missing to This Book (nothing here is changed) or Replace This Book (it asks first, then erases this book and restores the file).
How do I archive or erase a client?On her card, the ••• menu › Archive: she leaves your lists, search and reminders, and her visits and their money stay in your totals. Bring her back from Clients › Archived or Settings › Archived Clients. To remove her and her visits for good, choose Erase Her and Her Visits; it names how many visits go and asks first.
How do I delete everything?Settings › Delete All Data erases every client, visit, appointment, photo, expense, supply and export from the device. A backup file you saved elsewhere stays where you put it.
How do I export my clients and visits, or my money for my accountant?Settings › Export CSV writes your clients and visits as CSV files; their health columns are included only when you turn on Include health details. With Polish Pro, Money › Tax Year › Export for Your Accountant writes the year's takings, tips and expenses.
What do Free, Polish Pro and Polish for good include?Free: 15 clients with every visit they ever had, 3 photos a set, 20 inspiration photos, appointments, reminders, the CSV export and book backups. Polish Pro adds every client after that, every photo of each set, unlimited inspiration, Money (the month's bottle, tips apart, expenses and booth rent, the tax year), Supplies with a reorder list, set cards without the Polish mark, the year recap and two more widgets. It is a monthly or yearly subscription, or Polish for good: one payment, nothing renews.
How do I restore a purchase?Settings › See Polish Pro › Restore Purchases (also on every Polish Pro sheet). Polish for good and subscriptions both come back on a new device signed in with the same Apple Account.
How do I cancel a subscription?iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Polish › Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before the end of the period. In Polish, Settings › Manage Subscription opens the same page. Polish for good has nothing to cancel.
How do I ask for a refund?Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose the purchase and Request a Refund. Apple decides; we can't refund an App Store purchase ourselves.
I came from Polish 1.x. Is everything still there?Yes. Every client, visit and photo moved over, and What's New shows your counts once. Your 1.x amounts are labelled with your region's currency; if that's wrong, change it in Settings › Your book's currency (nothing is converted). The 1.x AI advisor is gone; the Refer, don't treat cards on a client's card take its place.
