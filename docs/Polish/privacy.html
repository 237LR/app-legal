Privacy Policy

Polish · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Polish is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Polish, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Polish is stored on your device: your clients, their visits and appointments, set photos, money, supplies and settings. Polish has no account and no server of ours, and nothing you enter is synced through iCloud. Set photos are saved without their location. Reminders are scheduled on the device; nothing is sent to us to deliver them. The widgets read a small summary the app writes on the device (first names, appointment times and services, and the short label of an allergy); it never leaves the device.

Your Clients' Details

Your clients are people who have not signed up to Polish, so their details are yours to keep safe. Their names and contact details, and their allergies and medical notes, stay on your iPhone or iPad and in the backup file you choose to make. They are never sent to us or to any service: not to RevenueCat, not to an AI service (Polish has none), and not to iCloud.

A book backup (Settings › Back Up or Restore) is one file with every client, visit, appointment, photo and amount, including clients' allergies and medical notes; the save sheet says so. It goes only where you put it: Files, AirDrop to your own iPad, or anywhere you share it. A CSV export (Settings › Export CSV) holds health columns only when you tick "Include health details". A set card you share carries the photo and its colour, with no name, date or place. Settings › Delete All Data erases every record, photo and export from the device.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Polish are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Polish, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Polish. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Polish contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Polish is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete the data on your device by deleting the app. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
