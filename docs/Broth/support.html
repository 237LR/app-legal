Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription?Open Broth, go to the paywall or Profile tab, and tap Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel a subscription?On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple ID, tap Subscriptions, choose Broth, and cancel there.
Where are the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use?Both links are available inside Broth on the paywalls and in the Profile tab.
Does Broth work offline?Core planning, timers, logs, and saved content work locally on device. The AI Ramen Advisor requires an internet connection.
Is my data backed up?Your data is stored on device and may sync with your private iCloud account when iCloud is available for Broth.
How do I delete my data?Use Profile → Delete All Data inside the app. If you signed in with Apple, you can also use Delete Account.
