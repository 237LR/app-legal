Privacy Policy

Iron · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Iron does not collect analytics or usage data, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and has no account system or server of its own for your records. Your bikes, service schedules and records, rides, tire records, modifications, gear, photos and notes stay on your device. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple passes anonymous crash reports to us.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Iron does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. Photos you add are kept inside the app at a reduced size. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name and email address Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show them in Profile. Service reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, and the PDF and CSV exports are made on your device and shared only where you send them.

AI Riding Companion (optional, Iron Pro)

The AI Riding Companion answers general questions about motorcycle maintenance, tires, gear and riding using xAI's Grok. Iron asks for your permission before the first question and sends nothing until you allow it. You can turn it off at any time in Profile > Settings > AI Riding Companion; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent: the question you type, exactly as you write it; the selected bike's year, make and model, its odometer reading (in the unit you use), and the oil type and front and rear tires you entered, so answers fit your bike; and Iron's standard instructions for the answer (general guidance, safety first, and the owner's manual has the final word on intervals, torque values and pressures). It is sent only when you tap Send or a suggested question. Your earlier questions in the chat are not sent again, and the chat is not saved.

What is never sent: your bike's nickname, VIN, photos, notes and purchase details, your other bikes, your rides, service records and costs, your service schedule, gear, modifications and tire records, and your name, Apple ID or email address. Leave out of your question anything you would rather keep private.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the answer; our server does not store it. Like any internet request, it carries technical details of the connection, such as your IP address, which our server currently passes on to xAI with the request. xAI processes the request to write the answer under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. Answers are general information and can be wrong; they are not your motorcycle's official specifications.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Iron are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Iron, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Iron. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Iron is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (your bikes with their photos, schedules and service records, your rides, tire records, modifications, gear and profile, and every scheduled service reminder), or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes the link to your Apple ID.
