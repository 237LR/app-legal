Privacy Policy

Gather · Last updated: September 24, 2026

Data We Collect

Gather stores the events, guests, menus, recipes, shopping lists, prep timelines, and hosting notes you create in the app. Subscription status is handled through Apple and RevenueCat so premium access can be restored across devices.

Data Storage

Your planning data is stored locally on your device. If iCloud is enabled, your data may sync through your private iCloud account using Apple's services.

Analytics and Diagnostics

Gather may collect anonymous product interaction and diagnostic information to improve reliability and understand which features are useful. This information is not used to track you across apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat processes subscription status and purchase history for app functionality. Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions.

No Tracking

We do not sell your data and do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Gather are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Gather, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Gather. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Gather is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete your local app data from within Gather or by deleting the app. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
