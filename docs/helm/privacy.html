Privacy Policy

Helm · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Helm collects no analytics or usage data, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and has no account system or server of its own for your records. Your boats and engines, engine hours, trips and fuel, maintenance tasks and service records, expenses and their receipt photos, checklists, notes and settings stay on your device. Helm has no AI feature and sends nothing you record to anyone. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple passes anonymous crash reports to us.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Helm does not sync it to iCloud or to any server, so it stays on this device only. If you use Sign in with Apple, the first name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in Profile; Helm does not store your email address. Service reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. CSV and PDF exports are made on your device and leave it only when you share or save them.

Maintenance and Safety Information

The starter maintenance schedules and intervals in Helm are general guidelines, not your engine or boat manufacturer's schedule: your owner's manual has the final word, and you can change any interval in the app. The pre-departure checklist and the man overboard reference are general safety information; they do not replace training, local regulations or instructions from the authorities.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Helm are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Helm, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Helm. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Helm is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (every boat with its engines, trips, maintenance tasks and service records, expenses and receipt photos, your checklists, profile and settings, every scheduled service reminder and the export files Helm made), or by deleting the app. A single boat and everything recorded for it can be deleted from its page. Profile > Delete Account also removes the stored name and Apple user identifier, and explains how to stop using Sign in with Apple for Helm in your iPhone's Settings.
