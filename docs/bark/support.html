FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?Open Bark and use Restore Purchases from either paywall or from the Profile tab.
How do I cancel my subscription?Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bark, then cancel the active subscription.
How do I delete my data?Open Bark → Profile → Delete All Data. This removes your local app data from the device.
How do I remove my Sign in with Apple connection?If you previously signed in, use Bark's account deletion flow in the Profile tab.
Where can I read the legal terms?Privacy Policy: privacy.html · Terms of Use: terms.html
