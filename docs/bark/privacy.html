Privacy Policy

Bark · Last updated: May 1, 2026

Overview

Bark helps you log cooks, recipes, notes, ratings, and photos for your BBQ sessions. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information Stored in the App

Bark stores the cook logs, recipes, cooker details, notes, ratings, and photos that you choose to add. This information is stored on your device.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Bark stores the Apple user identifier needed to remember that sign-in state on your device.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Bark may receive subscription status and purchase-history information needed to unlock premium features and restore purchases.

AI Features

If you use AI Pitmaster features, the prompt content you submit is sent securely to Bark's AI proxy service in order to generate a response. Do not include sensitive information in AI prompts.

Third-Party Services

Bark relies on Apple services such as the App Store, StoreKit, and Sign in with Apple to provide subscriptions and account-related features.

No Tracking

Bark does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Your Choices

You can delete your local data from inside the app using Profile → Delete All Data. You can also remove the app from your device at any time. Subscriptions can be managed in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Contact
