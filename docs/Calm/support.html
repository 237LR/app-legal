FAQ

How do I restore my subscription or Calm Pro for life?Calm 2.0: on Today, tap the person icon (Profile and settings) → Account and data → Restore Purchases; every Calm Pro screen has Restore purchases too. Earlier versions: Profile → Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel?iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Calm → Cancel.
Where is my data, and how do I move it to a new iPhone?Calm keeps your log on your iPhone: there is no account and no server for it. When you move to a new iPhone, set it up with Quick Start or from a backup of your old iPhone, and Calm's log comes along. You can also save a copy at any time: Profile and settings → Account and data → Your data → Export everything (CSV files). Deleting Calm deletes its log on that iPhone, so export a copy first if you want to keep one. Earlier versions also keep the log on the iPhone.
Delete my data?Calm 2.0: Profile and settings → Account and data → Delete All Data (also at the bottom of Your data). This erases Calm's records from your iPhone. Deleting the app also removes its data from the iPhone.
