Privacy Policy

Calm · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Calm is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Calm, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Calm is stored on your device.

AI Features

The AI AIP Guide (part of Calm Pro) sends only the question you type (at most 200 characters) and a fixed instruction text asking for a short answer about the AIP diet. Your check-ins, symptom scores, foods, challenges, conditions, medications, notes, name and Apple Account are never sent. Calm asks you before the first question and sends nothing until you tap Allow; you can switch the AI AIP Guide off at any time in Profile and settings > Settings > AI AIP Guide. Answers are general information, not medical advice. It goes to xAI (Grok), which writes the answer.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content. We don't add your Apple ID or email address to these requests, and we don't keep copies of them. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). Please don't enter information you wouldn't want to share with the AI provider.

We process this because you asked for it and allowed it (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and (b)); you can withdraw your permission at any time in Profile and settings > Settings > AI AIP Guide.

Online Lookups

After the AI AIP Guide has answered a question, at most once a day, Calm downloads a small settings file (the name of the AI model to ask) from our website on GitHub Pages (237lr.github.io). Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address with that request, under GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing else from the app is included.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Calm are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Calm, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Calm. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Calm contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Calm is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete the data on your device by deleting the app. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Calm sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
