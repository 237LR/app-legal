Privacy Policy

Balm · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Balm collects no analytics or usage data, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and has no account system or server of its own for your records. Your preparations, herb inventory, drying batches, supplies, formulas, notes and settings stay on your device. Balm has no AI feature and sends nothing you record to anyone. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple passes anonymous crash reports to us.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Balm does not sync it to iCloud or to any server, so it stays on this device only. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in Profile; Balm does not store your email address. Drying reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Herbal Information

The plant reference in Balm and the uses and doses you record are general information and personal notes, not medical advice. Check interactions with a pharmacist or doctor, especially in pregnancy. Balm does not send any of it anywhere.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Balm are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Balm, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Balm. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Balm is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (your preparations with their herbs, your herb inventory, drying batches, supplies, formulas with their ingredients, your profile and settings, and every scheduled drying reminder), or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes the stored name and Apple user identifier.
