Privacy Policy

Bench · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Bench does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server that stores your team's information. Your team members, 1:1 notes, feedback, action items, goals and profile are never sent to us. The only thing that ever leaves your device for our server is a request from one of the AI features, and only after you allow it (see below).

AI Coach and AI Review Prep

Bench Pro includes two AI features: the AI Coach answers management questions, and AI Review Prep writes a first draft of a performance review for one of your team members. They are off until you allow them: before the first request, Bench shows what would be sent and asks you to choose Allow or Not now. Nothing is sent before you tap Allow, and after that only when you tap Send or Draft Review. Opening a screen never sends anything.

What the AI Coach sends, each time you tap Send or a suggested question: your question as you typed it, with the names of the people on your Team list replaced by "a team member", and Bench's fixed coaching instructions.

What AI Review Prep sends about your team member, each time you tap Draft Review or Regenerate: their role, never their name (the request says "your team member", and names in what you wrote are replaced by "your team member" or "a colleague"); the strengths, growth areas and career goal written in their profile; their development goals with status and target month; from the last 6 months, the number of 1:1s and the moods and themes you tagged on them, how many positive and constructive feedback entries you logged and the text of up to 6 of them, and how many action items are open and how many were completed; and Bench's fixed instructions. The screen shows this exact text before you send it, and Bench adds the name back to the draft on your device. This is information about another person, your colleague: use AI Review Prep only as your workplace's rules allow.

Never sent: your team members' names, start dates, communication style, motivations and private notes; the text of your 1:1 notes, follow-ups and action items; other team members' records; and your own profile, Apple Account or email address.

Where it goes: the request travels over an encrypted connection to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI, the company behind Grok, and returns the answer to you. Our server keeps no copy. xAI handles the request under its own privacy policy. Answers are general guidance, not HR or legal advice; Bench does not store them, and they leave the screen unless you copy them.

You can turn the AI features off at any time in Profile > AI Features; while they are off, nothing is sent.

Data Storage

Your team data is stored on your device. When your device is signed in to iCloud, Bench also keeps it in your private iCloud database so it stays in sync between your devices; that database belongs to your Apple Account and we cannot read it. To keep your data on one device only, turn off iCloud for Bench in your device's iCloud settings. Notes you jot on a Prep screen stay on the device until you start that 1:1. The weekly prep reminder is a notification scheduled on your device, and the history PDF is made on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in with Apple is optional and only adds your name to your Bench profile. Bench keeps the identifier Apple provides on your device and in your private iCloud database; it does not use or keep your email address.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

xAI (Grok) — answers the AI Coach and drafts AI Review Prep, only after you allow it, from the text described above. Policy

Apple — payments, iCloud sync of your team data, and Sign in with Apple if you use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Bench are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Bench, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Bench. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Bench is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all your data in Profile > Delete All Data: it removes your team members, 1:1 notes, feedback, action items, development goals and your profile from your device and from your iCloud, and resets your AI choice. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account does the same and clears the stored Apple identifier; to stop Bench using your Apple Account, remove it in Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple. Deleting the app alone leaves the iCloud copy in place. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
