Privacy Policy

Stone · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Stone is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Stone, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Stone is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, it is also synced to your private iCloud account, which Apple operates and we cannot access.

AI Features

Some features in Stone use artificial intelligence. When you use one, your request - the text you type and the Stone information needed to answer it, such as your entries and statistics - is sent to xAI (Grok) to generate the answer. This happens only when you use an AI feature.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content. We don't add your Apple ID or email address to these requests, and we don't keep copies of them. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). Please don't enter information you wouldn't want to share with the AI provider.

We process this to provide the feature you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email address. They stay on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on); we don't run an account server.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Stone contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Stone is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete the data on your device by deleting the app and data synced to iCloud from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Stone sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
