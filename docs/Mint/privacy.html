Privacy Policy

Mint - Last updated: April 29, 2026

Data We Do Not Collect

Mint does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, VPN functionality, or proxy/tunnel services. The app does not install VPN profiles and does not route network traffic through a VPN.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your coin collection, want list, and local profile preferences are stored on your device. The current build does not sync this data to our servers.

Apple Services

Optional Sign in with Apple is used only to personalize the local profile on your device. In-app purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Children

Mint is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete your data in the app from Profile > Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
