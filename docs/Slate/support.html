FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?Open Slate, go to Profile, then tap Restore Purchases.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Slate.
Is my data backed up?Slate currently stores data on-device. Future releases may also support sync through your private iCloud account.
How do I delete my data?Use Delete All Data inside Slate's Profile settings. Removing the app also clears local on-device data.
How do I contact support about AI Study Advisor?Email the support address above with the app version and a short description of the issue so we can investigate quickly.
