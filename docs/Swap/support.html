FAQ

How do I restore a subscription? Open the app and use the restore purchases flow in the subscription area once it is available in your build.
How do I cancel? Go to iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Swap, and cancel there.
Is my data backed up? Data is stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled for Swap, it can also sync through your private iCloud account.
How do I delete my data? Remove toys, notes, and sets in the app, or delete the app to remove local data from the device.
