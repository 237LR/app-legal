Privacy Policy

Swap — Last updated: April 22, 2026

Overview

Swap is a toy rotation planner for parents who want calmer play spaces, clearer rotation rhythms, and better visibility into what their child actually uses.

Data You Enter

Swap stores the information you choose to add, such as toy names, categories, notes, rotation history, engagement logs, and profile details like a child name or age range.

Where Data Is Stored

Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple system frameworks. If you enable iCloud sync for Swap, your data is also stored in your private iCloud account through CloudKit.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to premium features, billing is handled by Apple through your App Store account. Swap does not store your payment card information.

Analytics and Tracking

Swap does not use third-party advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your personal data.

Children

Swap is intended for parents and caregivers. It is not designed for direct use by children.

Your Choices

You can edit or delete your content in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes local data from your device. If you use iCloud sync, you can also manage or remove stored data through your Apple account settings.

Contact
