Privacy Policy

Clasp · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Clasp collects no analytics or usage data, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and has no account system or server of its own for your records. Your supplies, designs with their recipes, steps and photos, orders, craft fairs and sales, prices and notes stay on your device. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple passes anonymous crash reports to us.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Clasp does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. Photos you add to a design are kept inside the app. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in Profile. Order and craft fair reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, and CSV exports of your supplies and orders are made on your device and shared only where you send them.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Clasp are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Clasp, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Clasp. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Clasp is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (your designs with their recipes, steps and photos, your supplies, orders, craft fairs and their sales, your profile and pricing defaults, a running bench timer and every scheduled reminder), or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes the stored Apple user identifier.
