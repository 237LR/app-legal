Privacy Policy

Phase · Last updated: September 24, 2026

Data We Collect

Phase collects anonymous analytics data (screen views, feature usage) through Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

Data Storage

Your personal data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is also stored in your private iCloud account.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Analytics (Google) — Anonymous analytics. Policy

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Phase are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Phase, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Phase. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Phase is not directed at children under 13.
