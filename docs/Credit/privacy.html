Privacy Policy

Credit · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Credit does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own for your records. Credit does not connect to your bank or to the credit bureaus and never pulls your credit report or score: the scores, goal, plan progress, notes, disputes and utilization calculator cards you enter stay on your device.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Credit does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in the app. Reminders (a reminder before a dispute's 30-day mark and the optional weekly check-in) are local notifications scheduled on your device.

AI Credit Coach (optional, Pro)

The AI Credit Coach answers general questions about credit using xAI's Grok. Credit asks for your permission before the first question and sends nothing until you allow it. You can turn it off at any time in Profile > AI features; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent: the question you type, exactly as you write it, and Credit's standard instructions for the answer (general information only, never promise results, never invent laws or deadlines). It is sent only when you tap Send or a suggested question. Your earlier questions in the chat are not sent again, and the chat is not saved.

What is never sent: your scores and goal, your plan progress and step notes, your disputes (bureaus, accounts, issues and results), your utilization calculator entries, and your name, Apple ID or email address. Your question may include details about your credit, so leave out anything you would rather keep private, such as account numbers or your Social Security number.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the answer; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the answer under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. Answers are general information, not financial or legal advice.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — writes the AI Credit Coach's answers from the question you type, only after you allow it. Policy

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Credit are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Credit, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Credit. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Credit is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (it also cancels pending reminders), or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes the link to your Apple ID. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
