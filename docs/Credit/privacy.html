Privacy Policy

Credit · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Credit is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Credit, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Credit is stored on your device: the items you add from your credit report, your disputes, letters and their dates, the scores you log, your plan, your notes and reminders, and the letter profile your letters are signed with (name, address and, only if you enter them, telephone, date of birth and the last 4 digits of your Social Security number; Credit never stores a full SSN or a full account number). If iCloud is turned on for the app (Settings › iCloud in Credit; on by default when the device has an iCloud account), all of it, the letter profile included, is also synced to your private iCloud database, which Apple operates and we cannot access.

No Account, No Server, No AI

Credit has no account and no login, and we run no server for it. Nothing you type is sent to us, to an AI service or to anyone else: Credit does not contact credit bureaus, creditors or collectors, and it does not pull your report or your score. Letters are made on your device; you print, mail or submit them yourself. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The sources Credit quotes (government pages, the bureaus' dispute pages) open in Safari when you tap them.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Credit are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Credit, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Credit. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Credit contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Credit is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

In Credit, Settings › Export makes a copy of everything you recorded (CSV files, your letters and the evidence binder PDF), and Settings › Delete All Data removes every record from the device and, when iCloud sync is on, from your iCloud. You can also delete the app, and data synced to iCloud from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Credit sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
