Privacy Policy

Clear · Last updated: October 7, 2026

Clear is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Clear, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Clear is stored on your device only. Clear does not sync it to iCloud or send it to any server of ours; the only thing that leaves your device is what AI Support sends, described below, after you allow it.

AI Support

AI Support (part of Clear Pro) asks your permission before it sends anything. Once you allow it, each message you send goes to xAI (Grok) to generate the answer, together with two details from Clear: your day count and what you are recovering from. If your message contains your name or your sponsor's name as saved in Clear, the name is replaced with "[name]" first. Earlier messages of the chat are not sent. Clear never sends your sponsor's phone number, your sobriety date, money saved, check-ins, journal, cravings and triggers, reasons, meetings, or your Apple ID or email address. Messages about drinking and recovery are health information, so please send only what you are comfortable sharing.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content; standard connection details, which can include your IP address, may reach xAI with the request. We don't keep copies. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). AI Support gives general information, not medical advice.

We process this with your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and, for health information, art. 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Profile > Settings > AI Support; Delete All Data also forgets it.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email address. They stay on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on); we don't run an account server.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Clear contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Clear is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete everything Clear stores with Profile > Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Clear sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
