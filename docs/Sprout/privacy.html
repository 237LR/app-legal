Privacy Policy

Sprout · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Sprout does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own for your records. The children you add (name and date of birth), the observations and activity logs you write, the name you may enter for yourself, your settings and the AI suggestions you have received stay on your device.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Sprout does not sync it to iCloud or to any server.

AI Montessori Advisor (optional, Pro)

The AI Montessori Advisor suggests one Montessori activity or approach for the week, using xAI's Grok. Sprout is used by parents, and it asks the parent for permission before the first request; nothing is sent until you tap Allow. You can turn the advisor off at any time in Profile > AI Montessori Advisor; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent, when you open the advisor and its last suggestion is more than a day old, and each time you tap Generate or Regenerate: your child's age (for example "22 months"), worked out on your device from the date of birth; the text of that child's 3 most recent observations, as you wrote them, with your child's name replaced by "your child" (anything else you wrote in them, such as another person's name, is sent as written); and Sprout's fixed instructions (suggest one activity for this week, practically, without diagnosing anything).

What is never sent: your child's name and date of birth; the tags, concentration, duration, dates and "what to offer next" notes of your observations; your activity logs; your other children's details; and your own name, Apple ID or email address.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the suggestion; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the suggestion under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. Suggestions are ideas for play, not medical or developmental advice.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily activity reminder in Profile > Notifications, Sprout schedules it as a local notification on your device at the time you choose. It is created on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — writes the AI Montessori Advisor's suggestions from what is listed above, only after you allow it. Policy

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments and notifications.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Sprout are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Sprout, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Sprout. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Sprout is used by parents to follow their children's Montessori activities; it is not directed at children under 13. What a parent enters about a child stays on the device, except the items listed under AI Montessori Advisor, which are sent only with the parent's permission and never include the child's name.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (this also deletes saved AI suggestions and turns the AI Montessori Advisor off until you allow it again), or by deleting the app. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
