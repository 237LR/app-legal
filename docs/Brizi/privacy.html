Privacy Policy

Brizi · Last updated October 30, 2026

Brizi plans a trip you can actually do, travels with you while you do it, and keeps it afterwards. It is made by Karol Jaworski (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions about this policy or your data: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

  • We store what you put into your trips, so it’s on your phones and on the phones of the people you travel with.
  • Your photos stay on your iPhone. Trip detection, recaps, reels and stamps are all made there.
  • Plans are drafted with the help of AI from details about the trip and its places, never from your name, your account, your home or your photos.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and your data from inside the app, at any time.

What we store, and why

Your account

When you start using Brizi, it creates an account with a random identifier, so your trips survive a new phone or a reinstall. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an identifier for you and, only if you choose to share it, your name. We don’t ask Apple for your email address.

Your profile

The name you show to the people on your trips, the colour of your avatar, your language, and your home city if you set one. Home is the city you pick from a list, stored as that city’s centre, never your exact address. It’s where your travel days start and end, and the starting point for weekend ideas. If you haven’t picked one, Brizi can work out a home area from where your photos were taken, rounded to about 5 km, and use that instead.

Your trips

Each trip’s destination, dates and time zone, how you’re getting there, its days and stops, your notes, the places you’ve saved for it (“the good stuff”), your votes, who is on the trip and their role, and invite codes. For a travel day: where it starts and ends, and roughly when you leave.

When you drop a link, a post or a screenshot into Brizi, only the places it found are saved to the trip, with the kind of thing they came from (a video, a screenshot, a Maps link…) and the link itself if you shared one, so you can open it again. Everyone on the trip can see them.

Places you save for yourself, outside a trip, are kept for your own devices only.

Plans drafted with AI

Brizi asks before it drafts your first plan with AI, and you can turn it off in You → Privacy, data and account; plans are then made without AI. When Brizi drafts or re-plans days for you, we keep a short record of each request so the free plan’s limit works and so we can fix problems. The drafted text is cleared after 30 days, and refused requests are deleted after 2 days.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your phone’s push token, whether it’s a test or App Store build, its language and time zone, and which notifications you want, so we can tell you when someone joins your trip, when someone else changes the plan, and when your trip starts tomorrow. If you use Brizi Premium’s live companion, the Live Activity on your Lock Screen has its own token so it can update when the plan changes on someone else’s phone; those tokens are deleted after a day.

Brizi Premium

Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app sends Apple’s signed record of your purchase to our server, which checks it with Apple’s certificates and gives Premium to your account and to the trips you host. Apple also tells our server about renewals, refunds and cancellations, so Premium stays correct.

Technical logs

Our hosting providers keep short-lived request logs, including IP addresses, to keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We process this data to provide Brizi to you (the contract between us), and, for logs and security, in our legitimate interest in running a safe service. Location, photos and notifications are used only with the permissions you give in iOS, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos. To find the trips you’ve already taken, and to make a trip’s recap, reel and stamp, Brizi looks at when and where your photos were taken, on your iPhone. Nothing from your photo library is uploaded; the only thing that can leave your iPhone is the rounded home area described above, if you haven’t set a home city. Saving a recap to Photos needs permission to add photos, nothing more.

Your location. Brizi uses your location only while the app is open. World’s “near you” sends the area you’re in, rounded to about 5 km. On a trip day, the Guide notices when you arrive at a stop so it can open that place, and remembers which stops you reached for your recap; that stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us.

What you drop in. Text and screenshots you share to Brizi are read on your iPhone, with Apple’s on-device model where your iPhone has it and Apple’s text recognition. To find the places they mention, only the place names are searched in Apple Maps. When you share a link, your iPhone opens it to read its title or caption, the way a browser would.

Reminders, widgets and the Live Activity use a small copy of today’s plan kept on your iPhone. Leave-by reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.

The people on your trips

A trip is shared with the people you invite. Everyone on it sees the trip, its days and stops, the good stuff and who added it, votes, and each other’s names and avatar colours. Anyone holding an invite link can see the trip’s name, destination, dates and cover before joining. Database rules keep a trip visible only to its members.

Who else receives data

We use these providers to run Brizi. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions), with servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Everything listed under “What we store” is stored here.
  • xAI (Grok) writes the titles and short notes of drafted plans, and one-line descriptions of places. For a plan it receives the destination, the dates and season, the pace you chose, how you’re travelling, places from our catalogue and the stops already on those days. It never receives your name, your account, your home, your location or your photos.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and Live Activities, Apple Maps (search, directions and map images, requested by your iPhone), App Store purchases and App Store Server Notifications. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the invite pages on ceanic.app, which receive the invite code in the link.
  • Open data: place facts and photos come from Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth, and travel advice from the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office. Our server builds the place catalogue from them without anything about you. Place photos load from Wikimedia’s servers, so your iPhone asks Wikimedia for them as a browser would.

Some of these providers (xAI, Apple, Cloudflare) may process data in the United States. Where that happens, data is protected by the safeguards in their data processing terms, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell or rent personal data, show ads, or share data for advertising, and Brizi does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists, so your trips and memories are there when you come back. Push tokens are removed as soon as Apple reports them as no longer valid, and when no phone has used them for 180 days. Live Activity tokens are deleted after a day, records of how often you used features that have fair-use limits after 2 days, sent notifications after 30 days, records of Apple’s purchase notifications after 180 days, and failed invite-code attempts after a day. Hosting logs are kept for a short period and then deleted.

Deleting your data

You → Privacy, data and account → Delete account deletes your account, your profile, the trips you host (for everyone on them), your place on other people’s trips, your votes and your Premium records, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. Places you added to other people’s trips stay on those trips, without your name.

Deleting the app from your iPhone doesn’t delete what is stored on our servers; use the option above, or email us.

Children

Brizi is made for adults planning trips. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU, the UK and California), you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy of it, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app (You → Privacy, data and account, including “Export my data”); for anything else, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and database rules let each person reach only their own data and the trips they’re on.

Changes

When what Brizi collects or shares changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
