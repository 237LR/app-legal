Brizi Support

Plan a trip you can actually do, travel with it, keep it.

Write to us: karoljaworsky@gmail.com. A real person reads every email, usually within two days. If something went wrong, the iPhone model, iOS version and what you tapped help a lot.

Premium, and who gets it

Premium unlocks every planned day of every trip, AI re-plans for any day, the live companion on your Lock Screen, and clean recaps and reels. It travels with the trip: when you host a trip, everyone you invite to it gets Premium on that trip too, at no cost to them.

You’ll find it at the top of the You tab. It’s a yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Restoring a purchase

On a new iPhone, open You, tap the Brizi Premium card at the top, then Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with.

Cancelling the subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: open the iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Brizi. Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Planning with friends

Open a trip and share its invite link. Friends join from the link without making an account, and everyone sees the same plan on their own phone.

Dropping places in

Share a link, a video, a Maps place or a screenshot to Brizi from the share sheet, or paste it into a trip’s “good stuff”. Brizi reads it on your iPhone and adds the real places it finds. Anything it can’t match to one place is shown as “Couldn’t place”, never guessed.

Your trips on every device

Brizi works without an account at first. To keep your trips on all your devices, sign in with Apple in You → Privacy, data and account. Your trips stay as they are.

Deleting your data

You → Privacy, data and account → Delete account deletes your account and the trips you host. Export my data on the same screen gives you a copy first. More in the Privacy Policy.
