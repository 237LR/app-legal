Data on Your Device

What you enter in Cue is stored on your device only. Cue does not sync it to iCloud or send it to any server of ours; the only thing that leaves your device is what the AI Coach se

AI Coach

The AI Coach (part of Cue Pro) asks your permission before it sends anything. Once you allow it, each time you tap Get Training Advice Cue sends xAI (Grok) one short request to write a training tip: the name and breed of the dog selected on Home (or "mixed breed" if none is saved) and two numbers, how many training sessions and how many behavior log entries you logged for that dog this month. Cue never sends the sessions or log entries themselves (notes, ratings, locations, triggers, reactions), your dog's photo, your other dogs, or your Apple ID or email address. The tips you receive are stored on your device.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content; standard connection details, which can include your IP address, may reach xAI with the request. We don't keep copies. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). A tip is general training information, not veterinary or behavioral advice.

We process this with your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Profile > AI Coach; Delete All Data also forgets it.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Cue are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Cue, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Cue. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Cue contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Cue is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete the data on your device with Profile > Delete All Data or by deleting the app. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
