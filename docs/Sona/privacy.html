Privacy Policy

Sona · Last updated: June 28, 2026

Sona helps you practise your spoken English. We built it to be private by default — your voice stays on your device.

Your Voice Recordings

When you practise, Sona records short audio clips to score your pronunciation. These clips are sent securely to our speech-assessment provider, processed in memory to compute a score, and immediately discarded — they are never stored on our servers. Recordings kept for your "before / after" vault stay only on your device and are never uploaded.

Data We Store

Sona works without an account. If you sign in (optional), we store, in your account, only the data needed to sync your progress across devices and power friend leagues: your practice scores, XP, streak, sound progress, collection, display name, and friend connections. We never store the audio itself — only the derived scores.

Account & Sign-In

Signing in uses Sign in with Apple or Google. We receive a user identifier and, if you allow it, your email — used only to identify your account. You can delete your account and all synced data in-app at any time (Profile → Delete account).

Where Data Is Stored

Account data is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union, scoped so only you can access your rows.

Third-Party Services

Apple StoreKit — handles subscriptions; we never see your payment details.
Supabase (EU) — authentication, database, and our secure function proxy.
Microsoft Azure Speech — scores pronunciation and generates the model voice; audio is processed in memory and not retained.
xAI (Grok) — generates practice sentences and conversation prompts from non-personal inputs.
Google Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Google.

No Tracking, No Ads

Sona contains no advertising and no third-party tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Children

Sona is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete your account in-app, or remove all local data by deleting the app. For any privacy request, contact karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
