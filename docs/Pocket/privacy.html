Privacy Policy

Pocket · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Data We Collect

Pocket collects no analytics or usage data and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. It has no account system or server of its own for your records. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple passes anonymous crash reports to us.

Data Storage

What you create in Pocket (each child's first name, age and avatar, jar balances and split, allowance settings, savings goals, earning tasks and transactions, and your settings) is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Pocket on your device, it also syncs through your private iCloud account, so your own devices signed in to the same Apple ID see the same family; we cannot access it. When you delete a savings goal, or use Delete All Data or Delete Account, Pocket keeps a backup on your device for 30 days (Profile › Recently Deleted) so a mistake can be undone; it is removed after that.

AI Money Mentor (optional, Pocket Pro)

The Money Mentor is an AI chat on the Home screen where your child can ask simple questions about saving, spending and giving. It answers using xAI's Grok. Opening it requires a grown-ups check, and the first time a parent is shown what is sent and asked to Allow or choose Not now. Nothing is sent until a parent taps Allow; the app refuses to send anything otherwise. A parent can turn it off at any time in Profile › For Parents › Parent Settings; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent, each time your child asks a question: the question your child typed or tapped, after Pocket has removed your children's names (whole words, any capitalisation), email addresses, phone numbers and links from it; an age range taken from the age in your child's profile (for example 6 to 8), never the exact age; and Pocket's fixed instructions (short, kind, age-appropriate answers about money only, never ask for personal information, and remind your child not to share any).

What is never sent: your child's name, exact age or avatar; jar balances, goals, tasks, allowance and transactions; your other children's details; and your own name, Apple ID or email address.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the answer; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the answer under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. Up to 30 questions a day. AI answers can be wrong, so please talk them over with your child.

Reminders

If you allow notifications when you set up an allowance, Pocket schedules a local reminder on each child's next allowance day. Reminders are created on your device from your own settings; nothing is sent to a server.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — answers the Money Mentor questions described above, only after a parent allows it. Policy

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, iCloud sync, notifications, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data. Pocket shows no ads.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Pocket are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Pocket, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Pocket. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Pocket is a family app that parents use together with their children. A parent sets it up and enters each child's first name and age; these stay on the device and in the family's private iCloud and are never sent to the AI. Children can use some screens alongside a parent, including the Money Mentor once a parent has allowed it, and its questions are sent only as described under AI Money Mentor. Subscriptions, Parent Settings, paying and changing allowance, and the Money Mentor sit behind a grown-ups check. Pocket has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile › Delete All Data (a backup stays on your device for 30 days in Recently Deleted, then it is removed), or by deleting the app. Delete All Data also turns the Money Mentor off until a parent allows it again and removes allowance reminders. If you signed in with Apple, Profile › Delete Account also removes the stored link to your Apple ID. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
