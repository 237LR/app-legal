Privacy Policy

Bisque · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Bisque does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own for your records. Your pieces, glaze recipes, firing logs and schedules, photos and notes stay on your device.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Bisque does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in the app.

AI Studio Assistant (optional, Pro)

The AI Studio Assistant answers general questions about glazes, firing, clay and studio technique using xAI's Grok. Bisque asks for your permission before the first question and sends nothing until you allow it. You can turn it off at any time in Profile > AI Studio Assistant; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent: the question you type, exactly as you write it, and Bisque's standard instructions for the answer (general studio advice, never call a glaze food-safe, remind you to test fired ware for leaching and follow the material suppliers' safety data, and give temperatures in the unit you chose). It is sent only when you tap Send or a suggested question. Your earlier questions in the chat are not sent again, and the chat is not saved.

What is never sent: your pieces, glaze recipes, firing logs and kiln schedules, your photos and notes, and your name, Apple ID or email address. Leave out of your question anything you would rather keep private.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the answer; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the answer under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. Answers are general information and can be wrong; they are not a safety test of any glaze.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — writes the AI Studio Assistant's answers from the question you type, only after you allow it. Policy

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Bisque are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Bisque, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Bisque. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Bisque is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data (your pieces, glazes, firings, photos, notes and the name on your profile), or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes the link to your Apple ID. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
