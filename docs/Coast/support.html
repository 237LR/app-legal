FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?Open the app → Profile → Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel?iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Coast → Cancel.
Is my data backed up?Stored locally. With iCloud enabled, syncs to your iCloud account.
Delete my data?Delete the app. For iCloud: Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage → Coast → Delete.
