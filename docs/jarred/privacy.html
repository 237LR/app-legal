Privacy Policy

Jarred · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Jarred does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own for your records. Your recipes, batches, pantry, expenses and altitude setting stay on your device.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device; Jarred does not sync it to iCloud or to any server. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are also stored on your device, only to show your name in the app.

AI Canning Expert (optional, Pro)

The AI Canning Expert answers general questions about home canning using xAI's Grok. Jarred asks for your permission before the first question and sends nothing until you allow it. You can turn it off at any time in Profile > AI Expert; while it is off, nothing is sent.

What is sent: the question you type; the earlier questions and answers in the same conversation (the chat is not saved and clears when you close Jarred); your altitude setting, if you have set one above 0 ft; the names of up to 5 of your own recipes (names only, never ingredients, times or notes); the number of batches you have logged; and Jarred's standard instructions for a short, general answer that points you to tested USDA and NCHFP recipes. It is sent only when you tap Send or a suggested question.

What is never sent: your batch details (dates, jar counts, seal results, notes), your pantry, your expenses, your recipes' ingredients and notes, and your name, Apple ID or email address. Jarred does not read your location; the altitude is the number you entered.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the answer; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the answer under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy. Answers are general information, not a substitute for a tested recipe or your local extension office.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — writes the AI Canning Expert's answers from what is listed above, only after you allow it. Policy

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Jarred are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Jarred, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Jarred. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Jarred is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also removes the link to your Apple ID. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
