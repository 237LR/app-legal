FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?Chalk 2.0: open the Family tab → Subscription → Restore purchases. Earlier versions: Profile → Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel?iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Chalk → Cancel.
Is my data backed up?From Chalk 2.0, your records sync through your own iCloud (private to your Apple Account), so they are on every iPhone and iPad signed in to it. Earlier versions keep them on the device. In 2.0 you can also save everything, free, from Family → Export everything: CSV files and every photo in one zip.
Delete my data?Chalk 2.0: Family → Delete all data. This erases Chalk's records on every device signed in to your iCloud. Or delete the app, then remove its iCloud data: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Chalk → Delete.
A lesson was done on another day. How do I record the real date?Chalk 2.0.1: tap the lesson (Planner → Week or Day, or in its course) and choose "Done on…", then pick the day. For a lesson already marked, choose "Change date…". If that day is already logged, the lesson joins it.
How do I catch up on days I didn't log?Chalk 2.0.1: Planner → Day, tap the date to open the calendar and pick the day, then "Log this day…". It lists the lessons the plan had for that day. Start with the earliest day: each day you log takes the course's next lesson. If the day says a course "starts" later, tap "Change the start".
Can I fix lesson titles I pasted?Chalk 2.0.1: open the course and tap "Edit lessons". Tap a title to rename it, swipe to delete a lesson or join it with the next one, or tap "Reorder". A list pasted from a book's table of contents gets "Tidy titles", which removes the dots and page numbers and rejoins titles split over two lines. It shows every change first and can be undone.
What happens to skipped lessons?A skipped lesson leaves the plan, and the next lesson takes its class. From Chalk 2.0.1 the portfolio lists skipped lessons apart from the lessons done.
Something is cut off when I print the portfolio.Chalk 2.0.1 prints long subject and course names, notes and lesson lists in full. To print every school day with its notes, turn on "Print every day" in Portfolio → Sections.
Where are the settings?In the Family tab (the house; it was Profile before 2.0): each child's school years, state and school week, plus iCloud, Export everything, the subscription, appearance and reminders. From 2.0.1, Family → Help and about → Email support writes to us directly.
