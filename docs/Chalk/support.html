FAQ

How do I restore my subscription?Chalk 2.0: open the Family tab → Subscription → Restore purchases. Earlier versions: Profile → Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel?iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Chalk → Cancel.
Is my data backed up?From Chalk 2.0, your records sync through your own iCloud (private to your Apple Account), so they are on every iPhone and iPad signed in to it. Earlier versions keep them on the device. In 2.0 you can also save everything, free, from Family → Export everything: CSV files and every photo in one zip.
Delete my data?Chalk 2.0: Family → Delete all data. This erases Chalk's records on every device signed in to your iCloud. Or delete the app, then remove its iCloud data: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Chalk → Delete.
