Privacy Policy

Marrow · Last updated: April 7, 2026

Data We Collect

Marrow collects anonymous analytics data (screen views, feature usage) through Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

Data Storage

Your personal data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is also stored in your private iCloud account.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Analytics (Google) — Anonymous analytics. Policy

AI Features and Data Sharing

Marrow includes an optional AI Advisor feature (available to Pro subscribers) that uses xAI's Grok AI service to answer raw feeding questions.

What data is sent: When you use the AI Advisor, only the text of your questions is transmitted to xAI through our secure proxy server. No personal information (such as your name, dog profiles, meal logs, or account details) is included in the requests.

Who receives it: Your questions are processed by xAI (the company behind Grok AI). xAI may use your questions to generate responses but does not receive your identity or account information. See xAI's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

Your consent: Before using the AI Advisor for the first time, you are asked to review and consent to this data sharing. You can choose not to use the AI Advisor, and all other features of the app remain fully functional without it.

Data protection: All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our proxy server does not store your questions or AI responses.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Children

Marrow is not directed at children under 13.
