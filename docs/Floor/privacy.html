Privacy Policy

Floor - Last updated: June 04, 2026

Data You Create

Floor lets you log movement sessions, mobility checks, custom routines, reminder preferences, and related notes. This information is stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled for the app, it may also sync through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service.

Purchases

Floor offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's StoreKit. Purchase status and basic purchase events may be shared with RevenueCat in observer mode so subscription access can be tracked and restored. Payments are handled by Apple.

AI Movement Guide

If you use the AI Movement Guide, your prompt and relevant conversation text are sent to Floor's app API so the app can return a response. Do not include sensitive medical information in AI prompts.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Floor stores reminder settings on your device and uses Apple's local notification system.

Data We Do Not Collect

Floor does not use HealthKit, location, camera, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising tracking. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

Apple StoreKit and iCloud - purchases, subscription management, and optional iCloud sync.

RevenueCat - subscription analytics and purchase status in observer mode. Policy

Children

Floor is not directed at children under 13.

Your Choices

You can delete movement data in the app from Profile, or delete the app from your device. If iCloud sync was enabled, you can also manage app data from iCloud settings.
