Privacy Policy

BloomCare · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

BloomCare does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server of its own. The records you keep about your clients (names and contact details, due dates, birth preferences, visit notes, payments and insurance claims) are never sent to us.

Data Storage

Your records are stored on your device. When your device is signed in to iCloud, BloomCare also keeps them in your private iCloud database so they stay in sync between your devices; that database belongs to your Apple Account and we cannot read it. To keep your records on one device only, turn off iCloud for BloomCare in your device's iCloud settings. If you use Sign in with Apple, the name Apple shares with the app and your Apple user identifier are stored on your device, only to show your name in the app.

Sharing

BloomCare shares nothing on its own. A visit note leaves the app only when you tap Share and choose where to send the PDF.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

Apple — payments, iCloud sync of your records, and Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in BloomCare are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in BloomCare, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in BloomCare. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

BloomCare is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data: it removes your records from your devices and from your iCloud. Deleting the app alone leaves the iCloud copy in place. If you signed in with Apple, Profile > Delete Account also deletes your data and removes your Apple ID details from the app. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
