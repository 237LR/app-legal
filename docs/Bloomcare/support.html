Your clients' records

Where are my clients' records kept?On your iPhone (or iPad), inside BloomCare. BloomCare has no account and no server, so we never receive your records. If you choose, BloomCare can also keep them in your own iCloud account so your iPhone and iPad show the same practice: Today → menu → Privacy and your data → Where client records live. Customers who used BloomCare 1.x keep what they had until they choose.
Is BloomCare HIPAA-compliant?No. In the app's own words: "BloomCare keeps your clients' records on your devices and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account. BloomCare is not HIPAA-compliant software, and Apple doesn't offer a business associate agreement for iCloud. If you bill insurance or Medicaid electronically, you are probably a HIPAA covered entity: keep client records on this iPhone only, or in a HIPAA-compliant system."
How do I back up my records?Privacy and your data → Encrypted backup file → Make a backup. You choose a passphrase (at least 8 characters) and BloomCare writes one encrypted file that you keep, in Files or wherever you share it. Nothing of yours is readable in the file, and we can't recover a forgotten passphrase. To restore it, choose a backup file on the same screen and type its passphrase; you see what it holds before anything changes, and what was on the device is saved aside first. Restore works while records live on this device only.
Moving to a new iPhone?Set up the new iPhone with Quick Start or from a backup of the old one, or make an encrypted backup file on the old iPhone and restore it on the new one.
Can I lock BloomCare?Yes: Privacy and your data → Lock with Face ID (Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode). BloomCare then asks when it opens and after a minute away, and is covered in the app switcher. The 3 AM card of whoever is on call tonight and the birth buttons on the Lock Screen still work while it is locked.
A client asks for her record, or asks me to delete it.Open her record → More → Export her record (PDF, CSV) gives you her whole record to share with her. More → Delete Client deletes her, her visits, payments, invoices and claims from BloomCare; export her record first if you need to keep it.
Delete everything?Your practice → Data → Delete All Data deletes every record on this device. If you chose to keep records in your iCloud too, that copy stays until you delete it in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud; BloomCare never deletes it.

BloomCare 2.0

I used BloomCare 1.x. What changed?Everything you saved moved over. The first time you open 2.0, "What's new" shows your numbers and lists what to confirm: due dates and on-call windows 1.x may have filled in, clients marked Fully Paid with less recorded, and claims marked Approved (now Pending until the payer answers). Nothing changes until you choose.

Subscription

How do I restore my subscription?Today → menu → Your practice → Restore Purchases, or Restore on any BloomCare Pro screen.
How do I cancel?Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BloomCare → Cancel Subscription.
