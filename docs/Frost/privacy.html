Privacy Policy

Frost - Last updated: May 2, 2026

What Frost stores

Frost stores your freezer inventory, reminders, and app preferences on your device. Frost does not use advertising SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Optional sign in and subscriptions

If you choose to use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide an identifier and account details according to your Apple settings. If you purchase Frost Pro, subscription and entitlement status are processed through Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat.

AI freezer guidance

If you use the AI freezer guide, the question you submit is sent to Frost's cloud proxy and model provider to generate a response. Do not submit sensitive personal information in AI prompts.

Data sharing

Frost does not sell your data. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate app features, such as Apple for sign-in and purchases, RevenueCat for subscription status, and the AI provider when you request AI guidance.

Your choices

You can stop using optional sign in and AI features at any time. You can remove local app data by deleting the app from your device. For support or privacy questions, contact karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
