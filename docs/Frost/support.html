FAQ

How do I restore Frost Pro? Open Frost, go to Profile, then tap Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel my subscription? On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Frost, and cancel there.
How do I delete my freezer data? Delete the app from your device to remove local app data.
Why is the AI guide not responding? The AI freezer guide requires an internet connection. If the network is unavailable, try again later.
