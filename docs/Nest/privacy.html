Privacy Policy

Nest · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Nest does not collect analytics or usage data, and it has no account system or server that stores your plans. Your events, guests and their contact details, RSVPs, dietary notes, tasks, menu, games, registry, budget and saved ideas are never sent to us. The only thing that ever leaves your device for our server is the text you type for AI Ideas, and only after you allow it (see below).

AI Ideas

AI Ideas (a Nest Pro feature) writes theme, menu, game and decor ideas for your shower. It is off until you allow it: before the first request, Nest shows what would be sent and asks you to choose Allow or Not now. Nothing is sent before you tap Allow.

What is sent, each time you tap Get Ideas: exactly the text you type in the "Tell me about your shower" box, and Nest's fixed instructions (a request for five shower ideas as a plain numbered list).

Never sent: your event details, your guest list and your guests' contact details, RSVPs and dietary notes, your registry, budget, tasks and saved ideas, and your name, Apple Account or email address. Only what you type is sent, so leave out anything you would rather keep private.

Where it goes: the request travels over an encrypted connection to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI, the company behind Grok, and returns the ideas to you. Our server keeps no copy. xAI handles the request under its own privacy policy. Ideas you choose to save stay in Nest with the rest of your plan.

You can turn AI Ideas off at any time in Profile > AI Ideas; while it is off, nothing is sent.

Data Storage

Your plans are stored on your device. When your device is signed in to iCloud, Nest also keeps them in your private iCloud database so they stay in sync between your devices; that database belongs to your Apple Account and we cannot read it. To keep your plans on one device only, turn off iCloud for Nest in your device's iCloud settings. The guest names, phone numbers, email addresses and mailing addresses you enter are stored the same way, only to help you plan; Nest does not contact your guests.

Planning With a Co-Host

If you invite a co-host (Nest Pro), Apple's iCloud sharing shares that event, with its guests, tasks, menu, games, registry and budget, with the people you invite, who see and edit it from their own Apple Accounts. You can see who has access, invite more people or stop sharing from the same screen. Shared data stays in iCloud; we cannot read it.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat — subscription status (see Purchases and Refund Requests below). Policy

xAI (Grok) — writes AI Ideas, only after you allow it, from the text you type. Policy

Apple — payments, iCloud sync of your plans, and iCloud sharing with co-hosts you invite.

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Nest are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Nest, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Nest. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

Children

Nest is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

Delete all data in Profile > Delete All Data: it removes your events, guests, plans and saved ideas from your devices and from your iCloud, including events you share with a co-host, and resets your AI Ideas choice. Deleting the app alone leaves the iCloud copy in place. Contact: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.
