Privacy Policy

Hydrangea (formerly called Nest) · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Hydrangea is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Hydrangea, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Hydrangea (your showers, guests, gifts, tasks, menu, games, budget and run sheet) is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, it is also synced to your private iCloud account, which Apple operates and we cannot access. The widgets and the Live Activity read a small summary of the selected shower (its name, day, countdown and counts, and on your Home Screen the first names of the households that haven't answered) that the app keeps on your device for them. Hydrangea has no account and no sign-in, and nothing you enter is sent to us or to any AI or other outside service.

People in Your Guest List

Your guest list holds other people's details that you type in: their names, phone numbers, email addresses, mailing addresses and what they told you about food. They stay on your device, in your iCloud and, if you share a shower, with the co-hosts you invite. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Hydrangea does not read your contacts. A reminder message to your guests is written and sent by you, from your own Messages or the app you choose.

Sharing a Shower with Co-Hosts

If you invite a co-host to a shower, the shower (its guests, gifts, tasks and plan) is stored in your iCloud and becomes visible to, and editable by, the people you invited, through Apple's iCloud sharing. If you join someone else's shared data, it is stored in their iCloud. We never receive it. The owner can stop sharing, and a co-host can leave, at any time; the shower then leaves the co-host's device, and the owner keeps everything. A co-host on your shower also uses your Hydrangea Pro for it; nothing about their own purchases is shared.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Hydrangea are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Hydrangea, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Hydrangea. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Hydrangea contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Hydrangea is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can export every record as spreadsheets (Settings → Your Records → Export All Records), delete everything with Delete All Data in Settings, or delete the data on your device by deleting the app and data synced to iCloud from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Hydrangea sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
