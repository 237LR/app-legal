FAQ

What is Hydrangea?Hydrangea (formerly called Nest) is a baby shower planner for whoever hosts: the shower's card and countdown, guests as households with their answers, gifts and who gave each one, the thank-you list, tasks, the menu, games, and the shower day itself. It is in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian, on iPhone and iPad.
I used Nest. Where are my showers?Nest is now Hydrangea: the same app with a new name. Updating keeps every shower, guest, RSVP, gift, task, dish, game and budget line where it was, and a subscription you had stays on.
Where is my data?On your iPhone or iPad, and in your own iCloud if iCloud is on for the app (your other devices with the same Apple Account see the same showers). Nothing is sent to us. There is no account and no sign-in.
How does co-hosting work?Shower tab → Co-hosts → Invite a Co-Host (Hydrangea Pro). Your co-host accepts with their own Apple Account and plans from their own iPhone or iPad; they use your Pro for that shower. Stop Sharing (you) or Leave (a co-host) ends it at any time: the shower leaves the co-host's device and you keep everything. Co-hosting needs iCloud turned on for both of you.
Can I get my records out?Settings (the gear in the Shower tab) → Export All Records writes every record as spreadsheets (CSV) with a README. With Pro, the thank-you list prints as a PDF with each giver's address.
How do I delete my data?Settings → Delete All Data removes every shower from this device and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what is on the device; iCloud data can also be removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Hydrangea.
Pro for good, monthly or yearly?Hydrangea Pro is a one-time purchase (Pro for good: pay once, nothing renews) or a monthly or yearly subscription. Every guest, gift, answer, thank-you tick, task, the menu and games are free for every shower.
How do I restore a purchase?Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. It asks the App Store for your purchases, so Pro for good and subscriptions come back on a new device.
How do I cancel a subscription?Settings → Manage Subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hydrangea → Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Pro for good never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.
How do I get a refund?Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose the purchase and request a refund. We can't issue refunds ourselves.
