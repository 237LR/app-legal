FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?Open Sprue → You → Settings → Restore purchases. This works for subscriptions and the lifetime purchase.
How do I cancel a subscription?iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sprue → Cancel. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.
Will my pile follow me to a new iPhone?Yes, when iCloud is on for Sprue. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, install Sprue and give it a moment to sync. You can also export a backup from Settings at any time.
A paint I own isn't in the catalogue.Add it as a custom paint: Paints → + → Add a paint by hand, with its brand, name, type and a colour. Then email us the paint and range so we can add it.
Are the paint colours exact?No. They are close approximations; every screen shows colour differently and pots vary between batches. Matches between brands are suggestions, so check them against your own pots.
What do scheme ideas send?Only when you ask, and after you agree: the project's name, game, faction, intended colours and goal, plus the names of the paints on your rack. Never your photos, notes or account. Details are in the privacy policy.
How do I delete my data?Sprue → You → Settings → Delete all data. If iCloud sync was on, you can also remove what's stored there from iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Sprue.
