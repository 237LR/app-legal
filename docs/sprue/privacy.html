Privacy Policy

Sprue · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sprue is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Sprue, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

Your models, projects, recipes, paints, painting sessions, notes and photos are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Sprue, they are also synced to your private iCloud account, which Apple operates and we cannot access. We don't run a server that stores your hobby data.

The paint catalogue is built into the app; browsing and matching paints happens on your device.

Camera and Photos

Sprue uses the camera only when you take a photo of a model or scan your paint rack. Photos you add are kept with your models in the app (and in your iCloud if sync is on). Cutting a model out of its background for the Shelf of Glory is done on your device with Apple's Vision framework.

Rack scan reads the text on your paint pots live on your device with Apple's VisionKit to find them in the catalogue. Camera frames are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

AI Scheme Ideas

Scheme ideas use artificial intelligence and run only when you ask for them, after you agree to what is sent. The request contains the project's name, game, faction, intended colours and goal, and the names of the paints on your rack. It does not contain your models, photos, notes, sessions, name, Apple ID or email address. It is sent to xAI (Grok) to generate the ideas.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content, and we don't keep copies of them. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). You can withdraw your agreement in Sprue's Settings at any time.

We process this to provide the feature you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), with your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).

Widgets, Live Activities and Siri

Sprue's widgets and Live Activity show a small summary (pile count, streak, what to paint next, the running session) that the app saves on your device for them. Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and Export

Share cards and exports (backup and CSV files) are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send them.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email address. They stay on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on); we don't run an account server. You can delete your account in Settings, which also removes the connection to your Apple ID.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Sprue are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an anonymous app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Sprue, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Sprue. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Sprue contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Sprue is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete everything in Sprue from Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app; data synced to iCloud can also be removed from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Sprue sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
