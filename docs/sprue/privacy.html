Privacy Policy

Sprue · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Data We Collect

Sprue collects anonymous analytics data (screen views, feature usage) through Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

Data Storage

Your personal data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is also stored in your private iCloud account.

AI Suggestions (optional, Pro)

AI Suggestions asks xAI's Grok for paint scheme ideas for one of your projects. Sprue asks for your permission before the first request and sends nothing until you tap Allow. You can turn AI features off at any time in Profile > AI Features; while they are off, nothing is sent.

What is sent: the project's name, faction, game system, planned colour scheme and goal (only the fields you have filled in), together with Sprue's standard request for paint scheme ideas. They are sent when you open AI Suggestions or tap Regenerate.

What is never sent: your models, photos, notes, paints, recipes and painting sessions, and your name, Apple ID or email address.

Who receives it: the request travels over HTTPS to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to xAI and returns the answer; our server does not store it. xAI processes the request to write the suggestions under its own privacy policy: xAI Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

xAI (Grok) — writes AI paint scheme suggestions from the project details listed above, only after you allow it. Policy

Firebase Analytics (Google) — Anonymous analytics. Policy

No Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Sprue are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Sprue, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Sprue. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.
