Privacy Policy

Flip · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Flip is made by Karol Jaworski ("we"). This policy explains what leaves your device when you use Flip, who receives it and why. Questions: karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Data on Your Device

What you enter in Flip is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, it is also synced to your private iCloud account, which Apple operates and we cannot access.

AI Features

Ask your numbers (part of Flip Pro) sends, with each question you type or tap: the question and the totals of the one period you ask about (a month or a year): the period's name, the number of sales and units, what came in, the cost of goods sold, marketplace fees, shipping labels, supplies and other item costs, car and trip costs, and what you kept. Only if you also switch on “Also send the period's item names and costs”: the names of up to 8 of that period's sales with what each sold for, cost and kept. Never sent: your name or business name, buyers, marketplace accounts, photos, notes, storage locations, your partner, your location, earlier questions or your Apple Account. Flip asks before the first question and sends nothing until you tap Allow; you can switch Ask your numbers (and the item names separately) off at any time in Profit > Privacy. After you allow it, Flip also reads a small settings file next to this policy (which AI model to use) at most once a day; nothing about you is sent with that request. Answers are general information, not financial or tax advice. It goes to xAI (Grok), which writes the answer.

Requests pass through our server, hosted on Cloudflare, which forwards them without storing their content. We don't add your Apple ID or email address to these requests, and we don't keep copies of them. The provider processes each request under its own privacy policy: xAI (Grok). Please don't enter information you wouldn't want to share with the AI provider.

We process this because you asked for it and allowed it (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and (b)); you can withdraw your permission at any time in Profit > Privacy.

Sharing with a Partner

If you invite someone to share with you, the data you share in Flip is stored in your iCloud and becomes visible to, and editable by, the people you invited, through Apple's iCloud sharing. If you join someone else's shared data, it is stored in their iCloud. We never receive it. The owner can stop sharing, and anyone invited can leave, at any time; each person keeps a copy of what was shared up to then.

Purchases and Refund Requests

Purchases in Flip are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status; RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides and an app user ID, never your name or the content you store in the app. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in Flip, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. By making a purchase, you consent to us (through RevenueCat) sharing the following with Apple for that request: how long you have had the app, how much of the purchased period has been used, whether the purchase was delivered, and the total amount you have spent and been refunded in Flip. We may also tell Apple whether we think the refund should be granted. Apple makes the final decision. We never share the content you store in the app.

No Analytics, Ads or Tracking

Flip contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Flip is not directed at children under 13.

Your Rights

You can delete the data on your device by deleting the app and data synced to iCloud from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete and move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page whenever what Flip sends changes; the date at the top shows the current version.
