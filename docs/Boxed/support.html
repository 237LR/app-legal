FAQ

Where are my moves and boxes kept?On your device, in Boxed's own storage. If iCloud is on for Boxed (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud), your moves, boxes, photos, steps and costs also sync through your private iCloud to your other iPhones and iPads signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple operates that copy; we cannot see it. There is no Boxed account and no Boxed server.
How do I get a copy of everything?Move → Settings (the gear) → Export everything. You get CSV files for moves, rooms, boxes, photos, steps, costs, essentials and the new home list, plus every box photo, as one .zip file or as the files. Each row keeps its id, so a spreadsheet can line two exports up. moves.csv keeps the from and to you typed: the export is your own copy.
Box labels and printingEvery box has a label with its number, its room and a QR code. The QR code holds the box number, the room and what you wrote is inside, as plain text, plus a link that opens the box in Boxed, so any phone camera shows what's in the box, even without the app. Notes and addresses never go on a label. Share or print one label free; Boxed Pro prints label sheets (A4: 2 × 4 or 3 × 7; US Letter: 2 × 4, 3 × 7 or 3 × 10, as Avery 5160) and the room-by-room box manifest as a PDF. Print at 100% (actual size), not "fit to page".
The legal steps in my planFor moves in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, France and Canada, the plan adds the official steps (forwarding mail, registering your address, your licence and car) with the deadline the source states, counted from your moving day. Each step shows its source, the words quoted from it and the date it was checked, with a link to the page. It is general information, not legal advice: rules change and depend on where you live, so check the source before you rely on a date. Other countries get the universal plan with "Check your local rules".
How do I delete my data?Move → Settings → Delete All Data. It deletes your moves, boxes, photos, costs and steps on this device and in iCloud, removes your boxes from Spotlight search and the widgets' copy of your move, and cancels Boxed's reminders. Your purchases are not affected. Deleting the app removes the data on that device only; data in iCloud stays until you delete it in the app or in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
I updated from Boxed 1.x. Where is everything?Everything you saved moved over: every box, photo, cost, step and note. The first time 2.0 opens it shows what changed, including a move day to confirm if one was saved late at night, boxes that had the same number, and that the AI assistant is gone (it never worked).
What does Boxed Pro cost, and how do I restore it?Boxed Pro is a one-time unlock (paid once, never renews) or a monthly subscription; the app shows the prices in your currency. On a new iPhone or iPad: Move → Settings → Restore Purchases. Yearly subscribers from Boxed 1.x keep their plan until they change it.
How do I cancel a subscription?iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Boxed → Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before the period ends. The one-time unlock has nothing to cancel.
RefundsPurchases are made with Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
